What is The Daily Dispatch? 📰

The Daily Dispatch is a news site for curious readers. Every story is chosen to help you understand what's happening in India and around the world — written in simple, clear language that anyone can follow.

Reading the news is one of the best habits you can build. It helps you understand people, places, science, sports, and why things happen the way they do. The Daily Dispatch makes it easy and interesting!

What is Ask Dispatch?

📰
Ask Dispatch — your AI news explainer
Every story has an Ask Dispatch button. Click it and an AI will explain the story in simple language and answer your questions — "What is uranium?", "Where is New Zealand?", "Why does this matter?". Ask Dispatch explains things simply, but it's always good to ask your teacher too!

How to get the most from the news

👀
Read the headline first. Does it make you curious? Good — that's the whole point.
📖
Read the description below the headline — it gives you the main idea in a few sentences.
🔗
Click "Read full story" when you want to know everything about a topic.
📰
Use Ask Dispatch to explain anything confusing. You can ask it as many questions as you like.
🌟
The best readers always ask "Why?" — keep that habit going forever.
